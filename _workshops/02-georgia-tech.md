## Overview

This workshop continues the refactoring of Macaulay2's C++ engine begun at
[The Future of Macaulay2](https://d-torrance.github.io/m2gt26) workshop and carried on at
[Refactoring the Macaulay2 Engine]({{ '/workshops/01-cornell/' | relative_url }}) at
Cornell in September 2026.

Participants will work directly on engine internals, so this is a workshop for people
comfortable reading and writing C++ and building Macaulay2 from source.
